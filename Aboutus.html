    CVR College of Engineering (CVRCE) is one of the leading private engineering colleges in Telangana. It was established by the Cherabuddi Educational Society, an NRI-promoted educational society registered in 1999 with the aim of providing quality technical education. The college was established in 2000–2001 at Vastunagar, Mangalpalli Village, Ibrahimpatnam, Rangareddy District, Telangana. It is affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad (JNTUH) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)

    Vision of the Institute

    To be a state of the art institution of engineering in pursuit of excellence, in the service of society.

    Mission of the Institute

    To excel in providing quality education at under graduate and graduate levels. To encourage research and innovation. To provide infrastructure and facilities to meet the latest technological needs. To establish Centres of Excellence through active interaction with industry. To nurture students towards holistic development with human values and ethics..
